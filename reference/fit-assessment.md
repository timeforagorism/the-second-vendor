# Ostrander's fit assessment

This is the document Ostrander sent Glenn last week. It answers each of the eleven items on his list. The item numbers match `billing-system.md`. Both people in the meeting have read it.

## Ostrander's answers to the eleven items

| # | Glenn's item | Ostrander's answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solar customers | **Standard** for monthly credit banking, which covers about 5,500 accounts. The 900 accounts on the closed 2011 solar tariff are paid their banked credit in cash each April. That payout would need custom work. **Ostrander recommends those 900 stay on the spreadsheet.** |
| 2 | Budget billing true-up | **Standard**, and correct across rate changes. The product trues up each account on the anniversary of its enrollment. Truing up every account in April would need custom work. **Ostrander recommends adopting the anniversary schedule.** |
| 3 | Rate changes | **Standard.** Rates are dated tables. A bill that spans an effective date is split automatically. Typical time to load and test at other sites: three weeks. |
| 4 | Time-of-use rates | **Standard.** Needs interval reads from the meter data system, which is on IT's side. |
| 5 | Correcting bills in bulk | **Standard.** Cancel and rebill by batch, with a preview before anything posts. |
| 6 | Reports | Aging and unbilled revenue are **standard**. Of the 14 month-end reports built in-house, 9 are covered by standard reports. **Ostrander recommends billing and finance redesign the other 5 around what the product provides.** |
| 7 | The nightly run | Sites of similar size run a cycle in under three hours. **Not yet tested on Harlan Valley's data.** |
| 8 | The notice clock | **Standard.** Each bill carries its own notice dates. A new past-due bill does not move an older bill's dates. |
| 9 | Duplicate notices | **Standard.** One notice per account per day, listing every bill that reached that step. |
| 10 | Third-party copies | **Standard** for one third party per account. About 600 of the 5,200 accounts name two. **Ostrander will build the second copy as custom work**, because the commission requires it. |
| 11 | Business mailing addresses | **Standard.** Separate mailing address and an attention line. Needs a new print file layout agreed with the print vendor. |

## What the document says about conversion and cutover

- All customers, accounts and open balances are converted. Bill history is converted for the last three years. Older history stays in the read-only archive of the current system.
- Two trial conversions, then three full months of billing run on both systems side by side, then a cutover weekend.
- The current system is kept ready for 60 days after go-live. Rollback criteria are "to be agreed."
