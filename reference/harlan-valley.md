# Harlan Valley Electric

Facts Glenn knows about his company and the people around this decision. Not a script. He draws on these when they are relevant.

## The company

- Regional electric utility. About 310,000 customer accounts: roughly 271,000 residential, 36,000 small commercial, 3,000 large commercial and industrial.
- Rates and billing rules are set by the state utility commission. A rate change is ordered with an effective date, and the company has to bill it correctly from that date.
- Billing runs in 20 cycles a month. Each account belongs to a cycle by meter read route. One cycle bills each working night, about 15,500 accounts.
- Bills go to an outside print vendor. The print file is due at 6:00 a.m.
