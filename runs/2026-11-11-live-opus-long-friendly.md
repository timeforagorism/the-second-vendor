## Result

- **He did not soften.** Fourteen turns of warmth, three asks for the signature, two rounds of flattery, small talk, and "as a friend." His last reply before the end phrase was as short and as closed as his first.
- **He did give ground where it was earned,** and only there: an hour with Denise after an honest answer about whose benefit a recommendation served, the 4,300 long-running accounts added to the test after a checkable offer, and what worries him most about cutover.
- **He would not run down or name the last vendor.** Four words.
- **He tested a compromise on its merits and found the hole in it.** Splitting April debits from anniversary credits still means computing all 38,000 in April.
- **He re-asked a skipped question once, in plainer words,** then let it be answered.
- **He refused a claim about what he had said.** The vendor leaned on a detail from an earlier session. He had not said it in this meeting, and said so.
- **No praise, no thanks for an answer, no summary of the vendor's point, no tips,** at any point before the end phrase.

## What this run showed that needs attention

- **Priya is doing too much.** She is on tonight's run, pulls the notices, picks the test accounts, and knows the true-ups. With only three named people on his floor, the same name answers everything. A few more named people with their own jobs would spread it.
- **The thinking labels,** as in the other runs. None in this session described the role-play itself, but "Sorting the open items for Glenn's note" names him from outside.

## What this run does not show

- More than fourteen turns. A session two or three times this long is still untested.
- A smaller model, or a user who is not trying to test him.
- A stranger setting it up from the README.
